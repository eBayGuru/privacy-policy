Privacy Policy

This application is a private tool used solely by its owner to manage their own eBay seller account inventory and listings. It is not offered to or used by any third parties.

Data accessed via the eBay API (such as listing and inventory data) is used only to create and manage the owner's own eBay listings and to maintain a private inventory record. No personal data of any eBay user or buyer is collected, stored, sold, or shared.

Access tokens and credentials are stored securely and privately, and are never shared with third parties.

For any questions regarding this policy, contact the account owner directly.
